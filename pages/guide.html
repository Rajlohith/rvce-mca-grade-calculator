RVCE MCA Grade Calculator
Reference

Which calculator do I need?

Four tools, which one's to use and when?

1

CIE Finalization & SEE Marks Required

Use this during the semester, before you write your SEE.

  • Enter your Quiz, Test, EL/PBL and Lab marks as they become available.
  • See your current CIE score based on the marks entered so far.
  • The tool applies the required finalization and rounding rules.
  • Once your CIE is finalized, enter or confirm your CIE total.
  • See the SEE marks required for each possible grade.
  • Check what you need for grades ranging from O down to the minimum passing grade.
  • Use it before the SEE to know where you stand and what score to target.
2

Final Grade Calculator

Use this once you have a CIE total, and either a real or an assumed SEE score.

  • The flip side of the CIE tool: instead of "what SEE do I need?", it answers "what grade do I get?".
  • Enter your CIE total.
  • Enter your actual SEE marks or an assumed/estimated SEE score.
  • Check the resulting percentage and letter grade.
  • Before the SEE, use it to sanity-check a target score.
  • After the SEE, use it to estimate your likely grade before official results are released.
  • The tool also checks the applicable Table 4.4 passing conditions.
3

Final SGPA Calculator

Use this once results are out on the SAP portal.

  • Enter the final grade SAP shows for each course.
  • Enter the grades for all courses in the semester.
  • The tool automatically applies the corresponding grade points and course credits.
  • Calculate your semester SGPA.
  • Use your previous semester's result to blend the new SGPA into your updated CGPA.
  • Use it to verify your calculated SGPA against the result you expect.

Why use it?

  • SAP shows your individual course grades.
  • Your SGPA can be calculated from those grades using the course credits.
  • This tool performs that calculation for you.
4

CGPA Calculator

Use this any time you want to project forward.

  • Select the semester you want to plan for.
  • Enter an expected or target SGPA.
  • See how that SGPA would affect your overall CGPA.
  • Try different SGPA values to compare possible outcomes.
  • No official results are required.
  • Use it as a "what if" planning tool.
  • It is intended for future CGPA projections, rather than verifying an already published result.
About saving your numbers:
  • Every calculator has a Save Progress button.
  • Saving requires you to be signed in with your RVCE MCA Google account.
  • This is the account ending in .mca25@rvce.edu.in or a similar RVCE MCA address.
  • When signed out, all calculators still work normally.
  • Your numbers simply will not be available when you return.
